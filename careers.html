Build Your Career at Prateek Finserve

Join a growing loans & insurance advisory in East Delhi. Learn the full file cycle, work with banks and insurers, and grow with performance-based incentives.

Why Work With Us

Prateek Finserve is a hands-on financial services firm — not a call-centre script factory. You will see real loan and insurance files move from login to disbursement or policy issuance, and learn how banking partners actually work.

  • Structured training on loan & insurance operations
  • Exposure to multiple banks, NBFCs & insurers
  • Performance incentives on top of fixed pay
  • Office at Sharma Complex, Shakarpur — in-person team
